About STUDENT HUB

Welcome to STUDENT HUB — a free online utility platform created to make everyday student tasks simple and convenient.

Our goal is to provide simple, easy-to-use tools that students can access from their phones, tablets and computers without complicated steps.

Our Tools

STUDENT HUB currently provides useful tools including Percentage Calculator, Age Calculator, CGPA Calculator, Marks Calculator, Attendance Calculator and Exam Countdown.

Our Goal

We want to build a useful collection of free student utilities that saves time and makes common calculations easier.

Free for Students

The tools on STUDENT HUB are designed to be simple and accessible, with no complicated setup required.
